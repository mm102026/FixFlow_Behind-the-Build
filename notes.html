Behind the build

FixFlow started in one garage.

It began as a prototype for Kehoe's Garage, a small car repair business where the phone kept pulling mechanics away from the cars. Every call meant downing tools to find out where a job was at, then getting back into the work.

01 The hunch

Lots of sectors need a generic version of this.

There's a great opportunity for different sectors and industries, particularly where there are different departments and customer communications involved.

Car garages and mechanics like Kehoe's would benefit, but so would almost any repair business:

Car garages & mechanics
IT & computer repair
Phone repair
Clothing & tailors
Furniture repair
Bike repair

Good for the circular economy. As demand grows for circular economy practices, FixFlow can help customers and businesses be more sustainable by encouraging fixing and repairing over replacing.

Customers

06 Product requirements

The PRD on one page.

Problem

Customers need updates on their repair automatically, without having to contact the business.

The demo shows how staff click a button at each stage of the repair, and how that's reflected in the customer portal when the customer enters their car registration or job number.

Audience

Car repairsPhone & laptop repairsBike repairs

Measuring success

60calls to the office today↓ Target: fewer
90 minof mechanic time today↓ Target: less

Any reduction in these would count as success. There's also an opportunity to cut repair times by accurately tracking when parts are expected, following up with suppliers and confirming orders.

Risks

  • GDPR: making sure the right consent is in place and that data regulations and laws are followed.
  • Adoption: staff need to be trained, use the system and keep job statuses up to date, so customers get accurate updates.

Out of scope

Payment systemsConnections to other softwareReal customer informationSending emails or texts to customers
